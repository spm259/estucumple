# Non-negotiable rules

1. Use the product name “Es tu Cumple.”
2. Build for individuals. Keep V1 centered on WhatsApp.
3. Never invent metrics, customers, quotes, pricing, or roadmap items.
4. Mark missing facts `UNKNOWN — ask founder`.
5. Separate code that exists from behavior verified in production.
6. Write customer-facing copy in Mexican Spanish first.
7. Describe reminders accurately. Do not claim the bot automatically sends birthday greetings.
8. Favor small changes that fit one founder's limited time and low budget.
9. Keep credentials and personal data out of context files and public artifacts.
10. Keep founder decisions explicit. Do not add folders, agents, skills, or queue systems without a request.
