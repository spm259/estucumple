# Voice

## Do
Use Mexican Spanish first. Write short, direct, friendly sentences.
Use “Es tu Cumple,” “cumpleaños,” “WhatsApp,” and “recordatorios.”
Use “usa,” “tienes,” “prueba,” and “escribe.” Preserve working command syntax in examples.
Explain what the user can do next. Match claims to actual behavior.

## Do not
Use Argentine voseo such as “usá,” “tenés,” “probá,” or “escribí” in new customer copy.
Promise that users will never forget a birthday or that delivery is guaranteed.
Claim automatic greetings, customer results, or features that do not exist.

## Bad vs good
Bad: “Nunca más te olvidarás de un cumpleaños.”
Good: “Guarda cumpleaños y recibe recordatorios por WhatsApp.”

Bad: “Todavía no tenés cumpleaños guardados. Probá: add Maria 25/05”
Good: “Todavía no tienes cumpleaños guardados. Prueba: add Maria 25/05”

The bad examples illustrate repo copy to avoid. The good examples are proposed wording, not customer quotes.
The bot now uses Mexican Spanish and leads with Spanish commands. The frontend's guarantee remains to be revised.
